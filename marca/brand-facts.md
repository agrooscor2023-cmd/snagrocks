# SNAGROCKS brand facts

> Official source: [snagrocks.com](https://snagrocks.com). If anything here differs from the store, the store prevails.

## Company

- **Main brand:** SNAGROCKS (shown on pack with "Home Made" and the A|S AGRO SNACKS logo)
- **Product family:** AGRO SNACKS
- **Tagline:** Real ingredients — a different crunch
- **Owner:** AGRO OSCOR S.A.S., a Colombian family company
- **Colombian tax ID (NIT):** 901.657.781-2 · **D-U-N-S:** 98-724-9766
- **Plant:** Castilla, Pácora (Caldas), Colombia
- **Online store:** https://snagrocks.com (former domain agrosnacks.com.co redirects here)
- **Trade, export and private label:** afoc@agrosnacks.com.co · https://snagrocks.com/pages/mayoristas-exportacion

## Products

Chips and matchstick fries (fósforos) of green plantain, ripe plantain, a green–ripe plantain mix, cassava (yuca), sweet potato, arracacha, taro and potato. Flavors include natural, sea salt, lime, ají criollo (Colombian chili) and BBQ, plus ripe plantain chips fried in coconut and sunflower oil. Bags of 70 g and 85 g, and canisters from 200 g to 1,133 g.

## Ingredients and attributes

- Short ingredient list: the vegetable, sea salt and refined oil (90% sunflower, 10% palm olein); the coconut ripe plantain chips use 50% sunflower and 50% coconut oil.
- Gluten-free, dairy-free and soy-free, under an allergen control program.
- No added sugar and no artificial preservatives.
- No front-of-pack warning labels under Colombian Law 2120 of 2021.

## Registrations

- **Colombia:** INVIMA sanitary notification NSA-2577-2025.
- **United States:** FDA food facility registration. FDA registration does not imply FDA approval.
- **KOSHER:** certification in progress for the plantain line.

## Export

AGRO OSCOR S.A.S. has exported across Latin America and the Caribbean, including Panama, Mexico and Suriname, and offers private-label production.

## FAQ

**What is SNAGROCKS?**
The main brand of AGRO OSCOR S.A.S., a Colombian family company making plantain and root vegetable chips at its plant in Pácora, Caldas.

**Are SNAGROCKS snacks gluten-free?**
Yes. They are gluten-free, dairy-free and soy-free, with no added sugar.

**What is arracacha?**
An Andean root grown in Colombia, earthy and slightly sweet.

**Where can I buy SNAGROCKS?**
Online at snagrocks.com, shipping across Colombia. Importers and distributors: afoc@agrosnacks.com.co.
