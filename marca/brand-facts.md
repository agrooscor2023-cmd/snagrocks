- Green plantain: chips and matchstick cut (fósforo)
- Ripe plantain: chips and matchstick cut
- Green and ripe plantain mix
- Cassava (yuca)
- Sweet potato (batata)
- Arracacha
- Potato: chips and matchstick cut
- Taro
- Andean mix of sweet potato, cassava and arracacha

## FAQ

**What is SNAGROCKS?**
A Colombian snack brand of plantain and Andean root chips, made by AGRO OSCOR S.A.S. in Pácora, Caldas.

**Are SNAGROCKS snacks gluten-free?**
Yes. The whole line is gluten-free, lactose-free and soy-free, with no added sugar.

**What is arracacha?**
An Andean root grown in Colombia, with a flavor between carrot and celery root.

**Where can I buy SNAGROCKS?**
Online at snagrocks.com (shipping within Colombia). Retailers, distributors and importers can contact the company directly.
