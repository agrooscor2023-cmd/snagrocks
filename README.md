# SNAGROCKS · AGRO SNACKS del campo colombiano

**Ingredientes reales — Un crunch diferente.**

SNAGROCKS es una marca colombiana de chips y fósforos hechos con plátanos y tubérculos del campo colombiano: plátano verde y maduro, yuca, batata, arracacha, taro y papa. La produce **AGRO OSCOR S.A.S.**, empresa agroindustrial con planta en Castilla, Pácora (Caldas, Colombia), y hace parte de la familia de productos **AGRO SNACKS**.

## English summary

**SNAGROCKS** is a Colombian snack brand made from plantains and Andean roots: green plantain, ripe plantain, cassava (yuca), sweet potato, arracacha, taro and potato. It is produced by **AGRO OSCOR S.A.S.**, an agro-industrial company with its plant in Castilla, Pácora (Caldas, Colombia), as part of the **AGRO SNACKS** product family.

Gluten-free, dairy-free, soy-free, no added sugar and no artificial preservatives; made with the vegetable, sea salt and refined oil (90% sunflower, 10% palm olein). Colombian sanitary notification INVIMA NSA-2577-2025; U.S. FDA food facility registration; KOSHER certification in progress for the plantain line. AGRO OSCOR has exported across Latin America and the Caribbean, including Panama, Mexico and Suriname, and offers private-label production. Online store: [snagrocks.com](https://snagrocks.com) · trade inquiries: afoc@agrosnacks.com.co.

## Convenciones del catálogo

- **Imágenes de producto:** 1500 × 1200 px. Principal = frente del empaque; secundaria = vista posterior.
- **Títulos:** `[Producto] [sabor] [peso] | SNAGROCKS`, igual a lo que dice el empaque.
- **SKU:** `MARCA-PRODUCTO-CORTE-SABOR-EMPAQUE+PESO-ACEITE-MERCADO`.
  Ejemplo: `SNG-PLV-C-NAT-BT70-GP-N` → SNAGROCKS · plátano verde · chips · natural · bolsa transparente 70 g · girasol y palma · nacional.
- **Código de barras:** cada variante lleva su EAN-13 junto al SKU.
